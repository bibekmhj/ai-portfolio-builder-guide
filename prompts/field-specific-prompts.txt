# Field-Specific Portfolio Prompts
# These prompts are designed for students outside of Computer Science.
# Upload your resume first, then use the prompt for your field.

=============================================================
BUSINESS / FINANCE / ECONOMICS
=============================================================

I've attached my resume. Please build me a complete, professional 
portfolio website as a single HTML file for a Business student.

Requirements:
- Professional, corporate design in navy blue and white
- Sections: About, Core Competencies, Work & Internship Experience, 
  Case Studies / Projects, Leadership & Activities, Education, Contact
- Highlight analytical, leadership, and communication skills
- Clean typography — this should feel like a Fortune 500 professional's site
- Mobile-responsive, all CSS included
