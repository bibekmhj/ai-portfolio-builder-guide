# Customization Prompts for Your Portfolio
# Use these after generating your first version with the master prompt.
# Copy and paste any of these into Claude to customize your site.

=============================================================
COLORS & DESIGN
=============================================================

Change the color scheme to navy blue and white with gold accents

Make the design minimal and clean, inspired by Apple's website style

Make the design bold and creative with a dark background and vibrant accent colors

Use a warm color palette — cream, brown, and terracotta tones

LAYOUT & SECTIONS
=============================================================

Add a Certifications section after the Skills section

Add a Publications or Research section after Projects

Add a Languages section to my Skills area

Add a Volunteering section before the Contact section

Move the Skills section above the Work Experience section

Remove the Education section and replace it with an Achievements section

=============================================================
CONTENT IMPROVEMENTS
=============================================================

Write a compelling 3-sentence About/Hero section based on my resume that 
highlights my unique value as a candidate

Rewrite my Skills section to group skills into categories 
(e.g., Programming Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Soft Skills)

Rewrite my Work Experience bullet points to be more impactful and 
results-oriented using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result)

Add a "Download Resume" button in the hero section that links to resume.pdf

Add subtle fade-in animations when sections scroll into view

Add a progress bar style to my skills section showing proficiency levels

Add a timeline layout to my Work Experience section

Add a working contact form using Netlify Forms

=============================================================
FIELD-SPECIFIC CUSTOMIZATION
=============================================================

--- For Business / Finance Students ---
Redesign this portfolio for a Business student. Replace the Projects section 
with a Case Studies section. Add a section for internships and leadership 
roles. Use a professional, corporate color scheme.

--- For Psychology / Social Science Students ---
Redesign this portfolio for a Psychology student. Add a Research section 
highlighting studies I've participated in or led. Include a section for 
academic publications or papers. Use a calm, trustworthy color palette.

--- For Design Students ---
Redesign this portfolio for a Design student. Make the layout itself feel 
like a design portfolio — visual, creative, and gallery-focused. Add a 
large Projects/Work section with image placeholders. Minimize text and 
let visuals do the talking.

--- For Pre-Med / Biology Students ---
Redesign this portfolio for a Pre-Med student. Add sections for Research 
Experience, Clinical Experience, and Volunteer Work. Use clean, clinical 
white and blue tones. Include a section for relevant coursework.

--- For Education / Teaching Students ---
Redesign this portfolio for an Education student. Add a Teaching Philosophy 
section. Include a Classroom Experience section. Use warm, approachable 
colors. Add a section for extracurricular activities and mentoring.

--- For Marketing / Communications Students ---
Redesign this portfolio for a Marketing student. Add a Campaigns section 
showcasing projects. Include social media and analytics skills prominently. 
Use vibrant, energetic colors that feel brand-savvy.

=============================================================

Add a favicon to the website using a simple emoji that represents my field

Add Open Graph meta tags so the site looks great when shared on LinkedIn 
and social media (use my name and a brief description)

Add a footer with copyright text, my name, and links to LinkedIn and GitHub

Minify and optimize the HTML file so it loads as fast as possible
